# Domain Overview

The Mirror Presence Layer defines the part of Mirror responsible for how two people can meet through a private Moment.

The interaction begins with an Invitation. A Human writes a short Gesture — a brief expression of presence, intention, or invitation that other Humans may encounter.

Another Human may answer that Invitation by sending a Response containing their own Words.

If the Invitation's author accepts the Response, the system creates a private Moment and records the Presence of both Humans within it.

The layer is responsible for the lifecycle of Invitations, Responses, private Moments, and Presence records. It also maintains the distinction between the technical User account and the Human represented within Mirror.

The layer does not determine which Invitations are shown to a Human or how they are ordered. Those responsibilities belong to separate future layers of the Mirror project.
