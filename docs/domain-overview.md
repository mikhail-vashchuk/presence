# Domain Overview

The Mirror Presence Layer defines the part of Mirror responsible for how two Humans can establish a private shared Moment.

The interaction begins in the public layer with an Invitation. A Human publishes a short Gesture — a brief expression of presence, intention, or invitation that other Humans may encounter.

Another Human may answer that Invitation by sending a Response containing their own Words. The Response is visible only to the author of the Invitation.

If the Invitation's author accepts the Response, the system creates a Moment — a private video conversation shared only by the two Humans. Until the Response is accepted, neither participant enters the private meeting space or gains access to the live conversation.

The layer is responsible for the complete lifecycle of Invitations, Responses, Moments, and Presence records. It also maintains the distinction between the technical User account used for authentication and the Human represented within Mirror.

The layer does not determine which Invitations are shown to a Human or how they are ordered. Those concerns belong to other layers of the Mirror architecture.
